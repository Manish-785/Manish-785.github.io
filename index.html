Research

Finding transition states with machine-learned curvature

Ongoing · B.Tech. project with Prof. Sudarshan Vijay, IIT Bombay · since May 2025

  • Nudged elastic band
  • MACE-MP-0
  • Hessian preconditioning
  • Safeguarded L-BFGS

To understand how a reaction happens, you need its transition state: the highest point on the lowest-energy path between reactant and product. The nudged elastic band (NEB) method finds that path, but every step costs force calls, usually from DFT.

I precondition the optimiser with the Hessian of a machine-learned potential (MACE-MP-0), projected onto the directions perpendicular to the path and paired with an analytical spring-force Jacobian along it, inside a safeguarded L-BFGS. The hard part was working out why curvature-based preconditioning breaks down near a saddle point, where the Hessian is indefinite and the two subspaces leak into each other.

The hot-season peak in maternal and newborn deaths in India

Manuscript in preparation · R&D project with Prof. Saket Choudhary, Koita Centre for Digital Health, IIT Bombay

  • Poisson fixed-effects models
  • ERA5 climate data
  • NFHS-5
  • 772 districts, 2009–2024

Births in India are seasonal, but the deaths of mothers and newborns follow a different calendar. Using district-month health records for 772 districts from 2009 to 2024, linked to ERA5 climate and NFHS-5 survey data, I show that deaths per birth peak every year in May–July, the hottest months.

Poisson models with district-by-year fixed effects compare each district only with itself. The peak survives five different birth registers as denominators, and the neonatal peak appears independently in the national household survey.

Projects

Spatiotemporal graph networks for multi-store forecasting

Preprint · Course project, EE782, with Prof. Amit Sethi · with Arpita Dayama · Aug–Nov 2025

Retail stores share trends, so forecasting them one at a time throws information away. We forecast weekly sales for 45 Walmart stores jointly with a spatiotemporal GNN that learns its own store-to-store graph, combining dilated temporal convolutions with graph convolution, and compared it with ARIMA, XGBoost and LSTM under identical features and splits. The learned graph recovered functional groups of stores without any location data. Result: 2.08% MAPE on Store 37, nearly 50% lower than ARIMA.

Quant Auditor: a small language model that audits trading backtests

Ongoing · Course project, IE624 Generative and Agentic AI · team of two, with Arpita

A trading strategy can look profitable only because of hidden mistakes such as look-ahead, ignored trading costs or in-sample tuning. I am building an auditor agent that investigates strategy code by running experiments on it, with one rule: no number reaches the user unless it is cited to its source or produced by an executed test. It is trained on 2,921 matched pairs of flawed and clean backtests on 20 years of real prices, using a fine-tuned Qwen3-4B on free GPUs. A hand audit found at least one major flaw in about 70% of 30 popular public backtest notebooks.

Generative AI for drug discovery

1st of 64,000+ participants · Google Solution Challenge 2025 · team project

A protein-conditioned recurrent network generates candidate molecules for a given target: a bidirectional LSTM with attention encodes the protein, and the generator is trained on 550K+ BindingDB samples. Candidates are ranked on drug-likeness, toxicity, solubility and predicted binding, using DiffDock and MolFormer. Generated molecules were 100% valid and 99.7% unique.
